Make email easier to review.

K-Mail by Jeff Kong helps you search connected mailboxes, review suggestions, and organize messages with explicit account and action controls.

What K-Mail does

  • Searches and displays email from accounts you choose to connect.
  • Suggests which messages to keep, archive, or review further.
  • Applies selected mailbox actions, including archive, Trash, labels, and read status.
  • Supports optional AI-assisted classification and draft preparation.

Why it requests Google access

Google sign-in identifies the connected account. Gmail permissions let K-Mail retrieve messages and labels, prepare drafts, and perform the mailbox operations you request. K-Mail's application interfaces do not provide sending or permanent message deletion.

Each account is connected explicitly through Google OAuth. Review suggestions do not by themselves authorize a mailbox change.

K-Mail is a privately operated personal application. This informational website does not connect to, display, or provide access to email accounts.

Optional hosted AI features can send email information to the configured model provider. Read the privacy policy for data use, storage, and account removal.
