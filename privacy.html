Privacy policy

K-Mail is a personal email application operated by Jeff Kong. This policy describes the Gmail integration and the optional review features of the current personal deployment.

Information accessed and its purpose

With your authorization, K-Mail accesses your Google account email address and Gmail information, including senders, recipients, subjects, dates, message identifiers, snippets, message content when requested, labels, and read status. It uses this information to search and display mail, classify messages, prepare drafts, and carry out selected mailbox operations.

K-Mail also stores account aliases, review choices, classification results, sender rules, and operational receipts so that reviews and actions can be tracked.

Storage and processing

OAuth tokens are stored in the operating system Keychain. Account indexes, review state, scan artifacts, and audit records are stored on the computer running the K-Mail instance. The computer operator's backup arrangements may create additional copies of local data.

Basic mailbox access communicates with Google. Optional AI-assisted review uses a configured local model or OpenAI through the operator's Codex account. Hosted requests can include email metadata, snippets, and other message information supplied for that review. Those requests leave the local computer and are processed under the provider's applicable terms and the operator's account data controls. This policy does not promise that hosted providers retain no data.

K-Mail does not include advertising or data-sale functionality. Email information is used for the email features requested by the account owner, not to build advertising profiles. K-Mail does not itself train a general-purpose model.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Hosted processing should be enabled only with settings appropriate for those requirements.

Retention

The current application expires inactive scan generations after 48 hours and places retired scan artifacts into a 24-hour quarantine before cleanup. Message classification and proposal records are generally retained for 30 days after their last activity or resolution. Compact audit records have a default 90-day retention limit. Cleanup is triggered by application startup and use, so it can be delayed while the application is stopped.

Account registrations, credentials, sender preferences, and durable rules remain until removed. Backups and external provider records follow their separate retention arrangements; local cleanup does not erase those copies.

Disconnecting an account and removing local data

You can revoke K-Mail's Google access in your Google account connections. K-Mail's remove-account operation deletes its local account registration, aliases, and saved tokens. Neither step automatically deletes all previously stored review records or backups.

To request removal of retained review data or help locating the appropriate instance data, contact jkglobal8@gmail.com. The operator will identify the relevant local records and handle removal and any applicable backup retention. Removing K-Mail's local records does not delete the original messages in Gmail.
